Frequently asked questions

Everything about converting PDFs to Markdown with this tool.

Is PDF to Markdown free? +

Yes. The core converter — uploading a PDF, OCR for scanned documents, previewing the result, and copying or downloading the Markdown — is free for up to 200 pages per month (resets on the 1st of each month). Need more? Starter is $1/month for up to 800 pages and Pro is $2/month for up to 2,000 pages. See the pricing page.

Can it convert scanned PDFs? +

Yes. Both scanned (image-only) and text-based PDFs are supported. Scanned pages are processed with OCR (optical character recognition) on the server, so large documents may take longer and run in the background. You can watch progress in the Tasks tab.

What file types and size are supported? +

PDF files only (.pdf). Very large files are rejected if they exceed the server upload limit, and extremely large documents take longer to process because OCR runs one task at a time.

Are my files stored? Who can see them? +

Files are processed on the server so you can preview and re-download results from your task history. Tasks are tied to the account that created them — other users cannot see your files. You can delete any task (and its files) yourself. See the Privacy Policy.

Why do I need to sign in? +

Sign-in keeps your uploads and conversion results linked to your account, and keeps processing jobs private. We use Google Sign-In and only request your basic profile (email, name, picture) — we never access your Google Drive or other data.

Does it keep tables and the table of contents? +

The output preserves headings and structure as Markdown, and tables are converted to Markdown tables. A table-of-contents panel is generated from the document so you can navigate long files. OCR results — especially for complex tables — should be reviewed before use.
